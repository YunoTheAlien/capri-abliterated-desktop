# The template pack

A drop-in pack that shows every kind of part, ready to copy. `night-owl.capri-mod` is the whole pack in one file.
Each file in `parts` explains one part on its own, with the lines to copy.

1. Copy `night-owl.capri-mod` and give it your own `id` (lowercase letters, numbers and dashes) and `name`.
2. Keep the parts you want and delete the rest. A pack needs at least one.
3. Drop it into her mods folder (**Mainframe > Mods > Mods folder**) or import it (**Import mod**). It is on at once.
4. Turn it off and everything it changed is hers again.

`slots.md` in her mods folder lists every slot she has: a file and a heading, or a whole file. The guide is
MODDING.md, "Drop-in packs". **Mainframe > Mods > Make a mod** opens the mod helper, which builds a pack like this
one part at a time and lays it on her with Reload; its Guide key opens MODDING.md.
