# Starter sounds

These ship with StoryBoard so a new project has something to try. Three of them,
Calm loop, Tension loop and Crash_001, are in the tutorial project a new StoryBoard
opens on, and Add the starter sounds in the library brings in all nine.

Every sound here has an author and a licence, and that is the rule: a sound nobody can
name the terms of does not ship. Four did until 2026-09-21, carrying only a note that
they came with the project, which tells whoever downloads this nothing about what they
may do with them. They were taken out rather than guessed at.
